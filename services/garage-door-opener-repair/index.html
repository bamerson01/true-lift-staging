The symptom is the starting point

Is it the door?
Or the opener?

The springs balance the door's weight. The opener moves the balanced door. A problem in either can affect the whole system.

Choose what you're noticing to explore the system.

How we find the fault

The door first.
Then the opener.

You don't need to troubleshoot it yourself. Our inspection follows the system from the door to the controls.

  1. Check the door first.

    We inspect the springs, balance and movement before blaming the opener. A door-system problem needs a door-system repair.

  2. Check power and sensors.

    We examine the supply, safety sensors and wiring for faults that could interrupt operation.

  3. Inspect the drive.

    We check the motor, drive components, rail and connections to see where movement is being lost.

  4. Test the controls.

    We test the wall control, remotes and keypad to isolate the problem and check the equipment you use.

  5. Explain the options.

    We show you what we found and explain the repair or replacement scope and price before work begins.

There's almost always more than one way to fix a door. We'll show you all of them.
