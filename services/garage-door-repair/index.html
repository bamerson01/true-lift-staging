Rough or uneven.

Tracks, rollers and the lifting system work together. Binding or uneven movement needs inspection, especially if a cable looks loose or damaged.

Gaps or worn seals.

Bottom seals and weather stripping help close the gaps around the door. Cracked or worn material can let in drafts, dust and water.

Not sure which service you need?

An overhaul replaces springs, bearings, rollers, cables and drums. Every part we install is new. We never reuse parts. The door panels and opener are separate; we explain what your door needs before you approve the work.

We inspect the condition of the system first. If the problem is limited to one part, we can quote that repair. If several lifting components are worn, we explain the overhaul option and let you compare the scope and cost.

Noise can come from rollers, hinges, hardware or other parts. An inspection helps identify the cause. A sudden bang, a heavy door, a loose cable or a crooked door calls for extra caution: stop using it and call for help. See broken spring repair.
