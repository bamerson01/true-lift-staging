What happens on the visit

A small service.
A clear conversation.

No pressure. No sales tactics. We explain what the door needs so you can decide on the next step.

  1. Tell us what's changed.

    A noise, rough movement or a concern about wear gives us a starting point. Tell us when it happens and how you normally use the door.

  2. Look at the system.

    We inspect the door and explain whether maintenance is appropriate or whether a repair needs attention. The condition of the equipment guides the recommendation.

  3. Maintain and explain.

    We carry out the agreed maintenance and talk through what we found. If more work is needed, you get an explanation and a separate quote to consider.
