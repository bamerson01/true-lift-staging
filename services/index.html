Broken spring repair

A broken or failing spring can make the door difficult or unsafe to move. True Lift will inspect the spring system and door balance, explain what failed, and provide the available repair options.

Do not attempt to lift or repair a door with a suspected broken spring. Call for help.

When one spring breaks we replace both — they share a cycle life, and the second is already near the end of it.

Common reasons to call

  • The door will not open or feels unusually heavy.
  • The door moves unevenly or will not stay balanced.
  • A spring has visible damage or a gap.
  • A loud snap was followed by the door no longer operating normally.

Garage door repairs

True Lift diagnoses and repairs common garage-door problems involving cables, rollers, hinges, tracks, brackets, seals, trim, and other door-system components.

The technician will inspect the system and explain the available repair or replacement paths before approved work begins.

An overhaul replaces springs, bearings, rollers, cables and drums.

Common reasons to call

  • The door is noisy, uneven, binding, or moving roughly.
  • A cable, roller, hinge, bracket, track, seal, or trim component appears damaged.
  • The door will not open, close, or stay in the expected position.
  • The cause is unclear and the system needs an inspection.

Garage door openers

True Lift diagnoses opener problems and provides repair, replacement, and new-installation options based on the condition of the opener and the garage-door system.

Whether a repair or a new opener makes sense depends on your door and the equipment you already have. We check that on site before recommending anything.

Every part we install is new. We never reuse parts.

Common reasons to call

  • The opener runs but the door does not move correctly.
  • The door reverses, stops, or operates inconsistently.
  • The wall control, remote, or safety system is not working as expected.
  • You need an opener for a new or existing garage door.

New garage doors

True Lift provides replacement consultations and professional garage-door installation. We look at the opening, the condition of the existing system, and what you need from your door.

Which door fits depends on the opening, the existing hardware, and how you use the door. That gets measured and discussed at the consultation before anything is recommended.

There's almost always more than one way to fix a door. We'll show you all of them.

Common reasons to call

  • The existing door is damaged, worn, or no longer practical to repair.
  • You want to change the appearance or function of the door.
  • A new construction or remodel needs a garage-door installation.
  • You need help comparing repair and replacement options.

Off-track & unsafe doors

True Lift helps with doors that are hanging, off-track, will not close, or may be unsafe to operate. These conditions require a careful inspection before the door is used again.

Stop using the door, keep the area clear, and call True Lift. Do not force, lift, or realign it yourself.

No after-hours fee. No emergency fee.

Common reasons to call

  • The door is visibly out of the track or hanging unevenly.
  • A cable appears loose, damaged, or disconnected.
  • The door is stuck open and the property or a vehicle is exposed.
  • The door looks unstable or could fall or move unexpectedly.
