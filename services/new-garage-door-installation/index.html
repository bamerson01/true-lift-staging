What you're comparing

Compare the door.
And the whole quote.

These are the details a new-door quote should name. What is included depends on the door and the work you approve.

  • The door itself.

    Model, dimensions, panel construction, finish and any windows.

  • Springs and cycle rating.

    The spring system specified for the door and your use.

  • Track and hardware.

    The rollers, hinges, tracks and other components covered by the work.

  • Opener work, if needed.

    Compatibility checks and any opener, controls or accessories in the scope.

  • The old door.

    Whether removal and disposal are included, and what the work covers.

  • The complete scope.

    Installation work, the total price and any exclusions explained before approval.

Every part we install is new. We never reuse parts.

How the consultation works

Your garage.
Your priorities.
A clear recommendation.

We start with the space you have and what you need from it. Then we explain the practical choices and the scope of the work.

Take the time you need.A new door is a considered decision. We explain the options so you can make it with confidence.

  1. Measure the garage.

    We look at the opening, headroom, side room and existing system. The door has to fit the space and work with the equipment around it.

  2. Talk about everyday use.

    Tell us how often you use the door, what you use the garage for and what you would like to change. Appearance, noise, insulation and budget all belong in the conversation.

  3. Compare practical options.

    We explain the differences between suitable doors and what each option changes. If the existing door may be worth repairing, we discuss that path too.

  4. Put the scope in writing.

    The quote names the door and the work, including hardware, opener work and removal where included. You can compare the details alongside other quotes.

  5. Choose when you are ready.

    Take the time you need. Before you commit, we discuss product availability and the expected installation timing for the door you choose.
