Service information and safe use

The descriptions, photos, videos and diagrams on this website explain common garage door problems and the services we offer. They are general information, not a diagnosis of your door or instructions for handling parts under tension. If a door is hanging, off-track or appears unsafe, stop using it, keep people and vehicles clear, and contact True Lift to check current availability. Do not rely on an online illustration or symptom description as confirmation that a door is safe to operate.
