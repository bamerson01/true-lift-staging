We explain what we find and the practical options. You approve the work before it starts.

  1. Get in touch.

    Tell us what the door is doing.

    Won’t open, won’t close, a new noise or a sudden bang? You don’t need to know which part failed. Tell us what changed and when you noticed it.

    Your appointment is not confirmed until Housecall Pro shows or sends a confirmation.

  2. Inspect & explain.

    See what we find.

    We inspect the door and opener, including the springs, cables, rollers, hinges and tracks. Then we show you what needs attention and explain why.

    Know the work. Know the cost.

A little preparation.
No troubleshooting.

These are helpful suggestions, not conditions of service. You don't need to diagnose or repair anything before we arrive.

If the door looks unsafe, leave it in place.

Off-track, hanging crooked or a suspected broken spring? Stop using the door. Don't force or lift it. Keep people and vehicles clear and call for help.

  • A little room to work.

    If it’s safe, clear a path inside the garage so we can reach the door and tracks. Don’t move a vehicle through a suspect door.

  • Controls within reach.

    Have the remotes handy and know where the wall control is. There’s no need to keep testing a door that isn’t working.

  • Keep everyone in the loop.

    Let whoever will be home know about the visit. Keep children and pets away from the work area.

  • Any repair history helps.

    A previous repair receipt or an idea of the door’s age can be useful. If you don’t have those details, that’s fine.
